Hofmann, H. (1994). Statlog (German Credit Data) [Dataset].
UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5NC77

Companion paper / original source:
  Hofmann, H. (1994). Statlog (German Credit Data). In D. Michie,
  D. J. Spiegelhalter, & C. C. Taylor (Eds.), Machine Learning, Neural
  and Statistical Classification. Prentice Hall / Ellis Horwood.

This dataset ships with a 2x2 cost matrix (see dataset README.md). False
positives (predict good, actual bad) cost 5x; false negatives cost 1x.
